Soroush Omidvartehrani's academic and professional background: where he studied, who he works with, what he has taught, and how to reach him.

## Education {#education}

Soroush is a Ph.D. candidate in Computing Science at the University of Alberta in Edmonton, Canada, where he started in 2022. His graduate GPA there is 4.0.

Before Alberta he studied at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad in Iran: an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from 2017 to 2021, where he ranked 3rd in his cohort and won the Best M.Sc. Thesis Award, and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from 2013 to 2017, where he ranked 2nd and was admitted directly to the M.Sc. programme without sitting the entrance qualification exam.

His coursework at Alberta includes Modern Database Searches (A+) and Introduction to Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining (A+).

## Advisor and research group {#advisor}

Soroush is advised by Dr. Davood Rafiei at the University of Alberta. He has been a member of the Data and Language Intelligence (DaLI) Lab in the Department of Computing Science since 2022.

Most of his published work is joint with Davood Rafiei, and several papers are also co-authored with Arash Dargahi Nobari, a colleague in the same group. Earlier work in Iran came out of the IP-PBX Lab (2019 to 2022) and the Cognitive Computing Lab, or CCL (2016 to 2019), both at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, where he worked with Dr. Mohammad Hossein Yaghmaee Moghaddam and Dr. Haleh Amintoosi.

## Industry experience {#employment}

Since 2025 Soroush has worked as a Data Scientist at Dodona Data in Toronto, Canada, alongside his doctoral research.

From 2019 to 2022 he was Project Manager at the IP-PBX Type Approval Lab in Mashhad, Iran.

## Teaching {#teaching}

Soroush has been a teaching assistant since 2014, and won the University of Alberta's Graduate Student Teaching Award twice, in 2025 and in 2026.

At the University of Alberta he has been a teaching assistant for File and Database Management from 2022 to 2026, supervised by Dr. Davood Rafiei.

At Ferdowsi University of Mashhad he was a teaching assistant and project supervisor for The Theory of Formal Languages and Automata (2015 to 2020), Computer Networking (2019 to 2020), Artificial Intelligence (2017), Fundamentals of Compiler Design (2016), Digital System Design (2015, as project supervisor), and Discrete Mathematics (2014).

## Skills and tools {#skills}

Soroush programs mainly in Python, Java, SQL, Bash and MATLAB.

On the generative AI side he works on LLM evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, embeddings and vector search, and LoRA fine-tuning. His data engineering experience covers Milvus and Zilliz Cloud, Spark, large-scale ETL and data wrangling, and web and table data extraction. His machine learning stack is PyTorch, Transformers, scikit-learn and pandas. On the operations side he works with GCP, Docker, Linux (LPIC-1 certified), HPC and SLURM clusters, and Git.

He speaks Persian natively and English (IELTS overall band 7).
