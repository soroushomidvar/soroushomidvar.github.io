## What this assistant is {#assistant}

This page answers questions using a small set of notes about Soroush's work, kept in the site's own repository. Each answer is written by a language model that is shown only the passages retrieved for that question, and every answer lists the passages it used.

It knows nothing beyond those notes. Questions about anything else — his opinions, unpublished work, private details, or topics unrelated to his research — will get an honest "that is not on this site" rather than a guess. It can be wrong about details even when the underlying notes are right, so the linked sources are the authority, not the generated text.

Good things to ask about: the research direction, any of the papers and how they work, the concepts behind them, his background, and awards.

## Code and data {#code}

His GitHub account is soroushomidvar. Code and datasets for GXJoin and WebTableX are released together in one repository covering both papers, and LDI and EdgeLM each have their own.

GXJoin is evaluated on two public transformation benchmarks: a web-table benchmark assembled from search-engine results, and a spreadsheet benchmark built from data-cleaning problems that users posted on Microsoft Excel forums. WebTableX reports on two real-world web datasets, one drawn from webpage content and one from organizational forums. LDI is evaluated on a mix of real and synthetic tables.

## Collaboration and availability {#collaborate}

Soroush is a Ph.D. candidate, so he is not in a position to supervise students. He is reachable for research questions, collaboration on data wrangling and LLM-for-data-systems topics, and conversations about his papers.

The contact card page of this site has his university email address, a downloadable vCard, a QR code, and links to his Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn and ORCID profiles. He is based in Edmonton, Alberta, and also works as a Data Scientist at Data on Motion Inc. in Toronto.
